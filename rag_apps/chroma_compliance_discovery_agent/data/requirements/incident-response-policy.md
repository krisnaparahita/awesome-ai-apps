# Incident Response Policy

## Regulator notification

Personal data breaches must be reported to the relevant regulator within 72 hours of the company becoming aware of them. The Data Protection Officer owns the notification.

## Tabletop exercises

The incident response team must run at least one tabletop exercise per calendar year and record the lessons learned.

## Post-incident review

A written post-incident review must be completed within 10 business days of closing any high-severity incident.
