# Access Control Policy

## Quarterly access reviews

System owners must review user access to every production system at least once per quarter. Each review must be signed off by the system owner and retained for 24 months.

## Multi-factor authentication

Multi-factor authentication is required for all employee and contractor access to production systems and to the identity provider.

## Offboarding

Access must be revoked within 24 hours of an employee's or contractor's termination date.
