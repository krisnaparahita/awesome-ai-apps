# Data Retention Policy

## Customer data

Customer content is retained for the life of the contract and deleted within 30 days of termination. Backups containing deleted customer content expire after 35 days.

## Application logs

Application logs are retained for 90 days. Security audit logs are retained for 13 months and stored in a separate write-once bucket.

## Legal hold

A legal hold from the General Counsel suspends all deletion for the data it names. Engineers must not rotate or purge held data until Legal releases the hold in writing.
