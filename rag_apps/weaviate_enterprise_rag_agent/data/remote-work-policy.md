# Remote Work Policy

## Eligibility

Employees may work remotely for up to three days per week after completing their first 90 days. Managers approve exceptions in writing and review them every six months.

## Working from abroad

Working from another country is limited to 20 working days per calendar year. Employees must request approval from People Operations at least 15 business days before travel, because payroll and tax obligations change by country.

## Equipment

The company provides one laptop and a 500 USD home office allowance, claimable once. Company data may only be accessed on company-managed devices.
