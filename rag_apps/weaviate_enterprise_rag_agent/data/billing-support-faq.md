# Billing Support FAQ

## Refunds

Annual plans can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase. Monthly plans are not refundable. Support agents may issue a goodwill credit of up to 200 USD without manager approval.

## Failed invoices

After a failed payment the customer gets three retries over 14 days. The workspace becomes read-only on day 15 and is suspended on day 30.

## Changing plans

Upgrades take effect immediately and are prorated. Downgrades take effect at the next renewal date.
